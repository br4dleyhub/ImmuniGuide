ABOUT IMMUNIGUIDE

Making vaccination data easier to understand

Immunisation datasets can contain large amounts of information, including countries, years, vaccine categories and coverage percentages.

ImmuniGuide transforms this information into simple, accessible results that make vaccination data easier to explore and understand.
